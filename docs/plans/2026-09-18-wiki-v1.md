# Wiki article v1: readable at a glance, detail on demand

Branch `feat/wiki`, worktree `.claude/worktrees/wiki`, dev server `http://localhost:3117`. This round redesigns the article route (`/wiki` and `/wiki/[slug]`) and its two rails. It adds no data and changes no generator: everything below is read from `src/lib/mock/wiki-data.json` as it stands. The page it replaces stays reachable, unchanged, behind a version switch, so the two can be compared.

## 1. Diagnosis

Before the first sentence of a page a reader passes five bands: a breadcrumb, a sentence about the dreaming job, the view tabs, an all-caps eyebrow ("WORKSPACE · TOPIC PAGE · WORKSPACE-SHARED") with a mono line of slug, version, run and time, and the summary, which the body then repeats on 23 of the 27 pages. A Topic's type is a 6px dot, and eleven types share six colours, so the colour cannot say which type it is; there are no icons. Every mention is the same dotted link (654 across the pages, one page links the same person nine times), and a citation is a 10px grey number whose only reading is the full-height drawer. The right rail opens on the raw Topic record. The Sources tab prints each atom's whole paragraph although every atom has a short title, and the index rail lists all 26 pages at once.

## 2. Principles

1. A Topic's type is an icon plus its colour, the same pair everywhere: the index, the header, the rail, links in the text. The icon tells the types apart where the six colours cannot.
2. The type chip sits above the title and a row of facets below it (who contributed, what is in dispute, how fresh it is). Engineering metadata (slug, run, version) leaves the reading column for an Info popover and the rail's collapsed Info section, still one click away for an audit.
3. Say it once. The summary leads only when the body does not already open with it, and a body's own "Also known as" line becomes the header's aliases.
4. Detail waits for a hover, a click or the end of the page. A citation previews on hover or focus and still opens the drawer on click; sources are one line each and expand in place; backlinks sit at the foot of the page with the sentence that links here. Nothing in the article body collapses: disclosure lives in the header, the rails, Sources and History.
5. The side panel is a map of the page: an outline with the section you are in, and everything the page mentions, grouped by type.
6. Brand tokens and the existing primitives only. Topic groups stay on `chart-1…6`; atom types and source kinds are ink icons, never a hue. The one new primitive is `ui/hover-card.tsx`.

## 3. The version switch

A segmented control at the top right of the wiki header offers **Original** and **v1**, v1 by default. The registry in `src/components/wiki/wiki-versions.tsx` is one list of `{ id, label, Shell }`; a v2 is one more entry. The choice is a `wiki-version` cookie (path `/wiki`, a year) written by a Server Function and read by the route when it renders, so it survives moving between pages and a reload without touching a single link, and the server renders the chosen design only, with no flash. The cost is that the article route renders per request instead of at build time, which static mock data does not notice. The original components change only where the switch has to appear (an `aside` slot in the header), where an import moves and where the group labels now arrive as props (below); none of it changes what they render.

## 4. Phases

Each phase ships on its own.

**Phase 1: types you can see, and a lighter article.**
- `topic-type.ts`: group → icon, dot and text colour; atom type → icon; source kind → icon. Presentation only, and it imports no data, so client components stop pulling the 1.7 MB store into the browser: `wikiGroupDot` moves here, group labels reach client components as props from the store through the server, and every client file imports only types from `src/lib/mock/wiki.ts` (`wiki.test.ts` keeps it that way and checks every group, atom type and source kind in the store has an icon).
- `TopicChip`: the type icon in its colour and a plain label on an outline badge.
- Header: the Topic's subtype as a chip, title, the body's "Also known as" names, facets (the current version's source day, "N in dispute", the assistants the cited atoms were extracted from), and an Info popover with the audit fields. The job sentence becomes "51 runs, Nov 2 – Nov 16" with its detail in a popover.
- Index rail: groups collapse behind their icon, label and count; the current page's group starts open; a search opens every group it matches.
- Sources grouped by atom type, one line per atom (number, title, source kinds, date, a dispute mark), expanding in place to the full claim.
- History as a timeline: version, day, first change note, `+n` / `−n` chips; the existing detail expands below.

**Phase 2: the rail as a map.**
- Outline with the current section marked as you scroll; an entry opens the Page tab if another tab is showing.
- Mentions: every Topic the page links to, grouped by type, as chips with mention counts, then a line down to the pages that mention this one.
- Info (collapsed): stats, the audit record, the Topic record and its history.
- "Mentioned in" at the foot of the page: each page that links here, as a chip with its sentence; four shown, the rest behind "Show all".

**Phase 3: citations at a glance.**
- The first mention of a Topic on the page carries its type icon; the first mention in each section is underlined as a link; later mentions in that section are links in plain ink that underline on hover.
- A citation opens a preview card on hover or focus: type, date, title, the claim in three lines, its sources. A click still opens the drawer.

**Phase 4: type and spacing.** Measured at 1456×868, the body was 14px with 87 characters a line, a section heading was 16px at 400, and a sub-section heading was 14px at 400, the same as a paragraph: a rule line above each section did all the work. v1 now reads on the brand's own scale.
- Body at `text-base` (16/24, about 70 characters a line); headings run 32 / 24 / 18 over it (`text-3xl` title, `text-2xl` section, `text-heading-lg` sub-section), all at the heading weight. A 20px section over an 18px sub-section read as one level, so sections take 24.
- Rhythm from the spacing tokens, not one flat gap: 12px between blocks (`mt-group`), 8px under a heading, 24px above a sub-section (`mt-stack`) and 40px above a section (`mt-section`). The rule lines above sections are gone; "Mentioned in" keeps its line as the page's footer.
- The column caps at `max-w-xl` (36rem, about 68 characters). `max-w-prose` does not work here: 65ch of Geist is 690px.
- Smaller things follow: the lede at `text-lg`, the facet line and the backlink sentences at `text-md`, strong in copy at `font-strong`, inline code at `text-label-14-mono`, the citation number at `text-label-12-mono`, `text-pretty` on paragraphs and `text-balance` on headings.
- The view tabs hide while Pages is the only view (`WikiTabs hideAlone`) and come back with the second one. The original design keeps its bar.
- The rails. The index's group rows go to 500, as the app sidebar's rows beside them are, and their pages stay 400 in the muted ink. Mentions showed every chip: 26 chips on 19 lines on the workspace page, 708px, so the rail ran past the screen and hid Mentioned in and Info. Each type now shows its three most mentioned Topics and the rest open in place behind "+N more". The caps section labels stay, as the app sidebar's do.
- The right rail at the left rail's size. Its outline had gone to 13px and its chips were the badge's 12px, the smallest text on the page beside a 14px index and a 16px body. The outline and "Mentioned in" rows are now `text-sm` like the index rows; the mention chips (and the ones under "Mentioned in") are `TopicChip size="md"`, 24px at 13px with a 14px icon; the Info rows and the Topic history notes are `text-md`. The header's inline chips stay 20px beside the dispute pill. The cost is height: more pages now scroll the rail on its own, which the three-per-type cap keeps short.

**Not in v1:** collapsible sections and lead cards in the body, link preview cards, a side peek panel for linked pages, and real topical tags (the store has none; the facets stand in for them).

## 5. Data

Every value v1 shows is a field of `src/lib/mock/wiki-data.json`, read through selectors in `src/lib/mock/wiki.ts`, or is computed from those fields (counts, the latest version's source day, mention groups, word counts). Nothing is typed in to stand in for store data, and the JSON is never edited by hand; an idea that needs a field the store lacks is dropped, not faked.

## 6. Gates

`npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run lint`, `npm test`, `npm run build`, and after the build `grep -rl supersededById .next/static` finds nothing. Every wiki route answers 200 in both versions.
